Miffy & Pop / Pop Exclusive

Our De Wallen store attracts loyal international visitors rather than spontaneous shoppers. But fans often compared prices with their home markets, and without tax-free shopping, they needed another reason to buy directly.

I proposed exclusive products for Pop’s own retail channels, turning a store visit into an opportunity to take home something unavailable at local retailers. The idea was adopted for Miffy & Pop.

From insight to sales

Exclusive colourways sold quickly despite higher initial stock than comparable non-exclusive colourways. I saw exclusivity influence undecided customers on the shop floor.

A different audience

Miffy attracted a broader international and female audience. I coordinated newsletters, retail activation, marketing assets, press outreach and personalised gifting.

08 / Commercial & operational experience

Behind every launch,
there’s a system.

Retail observations into action

During festival season I brought relevant previous-season products back onto the floor. During ADE I increased stock of caps, T-shirts and Amsterdam-branded items. When carryover products gained popularity, I brought in previous colourways.

Equipping retailers

I distributed product imagery, lookbooks, campaign films, press releases, social assets and launch guidelines to global retailers, coordinating embargoes and requesting removal of premature posts when needed.

One product. One consistent story.

I authored product descriptions covering materials, fit, functionality, construction and product categories. Once approved by our Creative Director, these became the official product information distributed across our webshop, global retail network and press communications.

Systems that outlast me

I created and handed over manuals for events, lookbook and product photography, retail, product descriptions, samples, styling requests, archives and PR. Good processes shouldn’t depend on one person.
